Record R², MAE. 2. **Experiment 2:** Train on 2022–2025 only, test on 2022–2025 (GroupKFold). Record R², MAE. 3. **Experiment 3:** Train on 2019–2021, test on 2022–2025 (cross-era). Expect significant performance drop. 4. **Statistical test:** Compare R²/MAE between Experiment 1 and 2 using paired t-test or Wilcoxon signed-rank test across folds. 5. **Residual analysis:** Plot residuals by year; check for systematic bias in Experiment 1 (2019–2021 data pulling predictions away from 2022–2025 reality). ### **What would constitute convincing evidence** - **Degradation slope distributions:** KS test p < 0.01 between 2019–2021 and 2022–2025. - **Era interaction effect:** `TyreAge × Era` term significant (p < 0.001) in mixed model. - **Cross-era performance drop:** R² drops by >0.15 when training on 2019–2021 and testing on 2022–2025 vs within-era validation. - **Residual bias:** Mean residual for 2022–2025 test set shifts by >0.2s when 2019–2021 data is included in training. - **Plots:** Tyre-age vs lap-time curves showing visibly different slopes by era; residual distributions by era. ### **Potential hackathon weakness** Judges may challenge: 1. **Fuel-load confounding:** You do not have actual fuel loads; lap-time drop-off conflates tyre degradation and fuel burn. Mitigation: Use `SessionType` (Race vs Qualifying) as proxy; acknowledge limitation explicitly.
